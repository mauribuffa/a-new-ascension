    About

    A New Ascension is an alt-rock project out of Jacksonville, Florida that launched in 2005. After two decades of a deliberately limited discography, it released its first full-length album, The Implicate Order, in 2026 — eleven tracks produced by My Platinum Sound at EWM Studios.

    Tagline, and mission statement: Rock for Nerds.
